Selected work

Keystroke authentication

People type with a rhythm that is hard to fake. I compared a CNN-LSTM against a Behavioral Transformer Encoder (BTE) for verifying users from typing timings on the CMU keystroke dataset.

The key finding: the Transformer wins once a user has enrolled 20 or more typing samples, but the CNN-LSTM is better at 10. That turns into concrete guidance on which model to deploy depending on how much enrollment data a product can collect.

The model is exported to ONNX and served through FastAPI, so it runs as a live verification service rather than a notebook.
